# Submission Guide

1. `01_Group_Member_Code/PanthersPlayers2026.java`
   - Original group member submission.
   - Do not claim this code as your individual contribution.

2. `02_Alani_Working_Draft/`
   - Your development version.
   - Keep this version if your instructor wants development history.

3. `03_Alani_Clean_Final/`
   - Your cleaned contribution.
   - This is the version to use when demonstrating your completed contribution.

The working draft and final version intentionally use the same basic functionality,
but the working draft includes development notes while the final version is cleaned.
